# SVP First 5 Minutes

Do not begin with Admin, architecture or integrations.

## 1. Dashboard — 45 seconds

Open with:

> PursuitNova is JSAN's pursuit-to-revenue platform. The first screen tells leadership what should not require another status call: are we on target, what are we committing, do we have enough coverage, and where does execution need intervention?

Show:

- Open Pipeline
- Commit
- Closed Won
- Execution Risk
- six-quarter revenue momentum
- Forecast Confidence
- Leadership Attention

## 2. Prospect → Lead 360 — 90 seconds

Open a Hot Prospect.

Show:

- Company + multiple Contacts
- individual owner
- source / geography / follow-up
- Meeting
- MoM
- Action Tracker
- relationship Timeline

## 3. Opportunity Pipeline — 60 seconds

Show Board view and open one opportunity.

Show:

- stage
- value
- probability
- Best Case / Commit
- follow-up history
- mandatory Won/Lost/Hold controls

## 4. Forecast — 45 seconds

Show Target → Pipeline → Best Case → Commit → Won and seller roll-up in corporate reporting currency.

## 5. Leadership Analytics — 30 seconds

Switch Q / H1 / H2 and show Vertical, Source and Owner performance.

Close with:

> Every relationship has an owner. Every meeting becomes an outcome. Every outcome becomes an action. Every opportunity carries a forecast. Leadership sees the truth in one place.
